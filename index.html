About Jagger

Where Tradition Meets Modernity

Founded in the heart of Nizamabad, Jagger represents the pinnacle of men's bespoke tailoring. Our master tailors combine generations of traditional craftsmanship with contemporary design sensibilities to create garments that are truly one-of-a-kind.

Every piece that leaves our workshop tells a story — of premium fabrics sourced from the world's finest mills, of precise measurements taken with meticulous care, and of countless hours of handwork that transform cloth into confidence.

Our Process

From Vision to Reality

A seamless journey from your first consultation to the final fitting.

01

Consultation

Discuss your vision, occasion, and preferences. Our style advisors guide you through fabrics, cuts, and details.

02

Measurement

Precise 30+ point measurements taken by our master tailor. Posture, asymmetry, and preferences all recorded.

03

Pattern Creation

Your unique paper pattern is hand-drafted. No blocks, no shortcuts — a pattern made exclusively for you.

04

First Fitting

Basted garment fitting to assess structure, balance, and drape. Adjustments marked for perfection.

05

Final Assembly

Hand-finishing: hand-stitched lapels, working buttonholes, hand-set sleeves. 40+ hours of craftsmanship.

06

Delivery

Final fitting, pressing, and presentation in our signature garment bag. Lifetime alteration support included.
